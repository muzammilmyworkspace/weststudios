01How do we start?

Walk to the end of the Westside and fill in the project brief, or email hello@thewestsidestudios.com. We reply with questions, a plan and a quote.

02Do you only build websites?

No. Website development is where we started, but we also run performance marketing, creative, branding, social media, SEO / AEO and e-commerce, together or one at a time.

03How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. We agree the timeline with you before any work starts and share progress as we go.

04How is pricing decided?

Every project is quoted on its scope. Share your budget range in the brief and we’ll shape a plan around it.

05Can you work with our existing site or ad accounts?

Yes. We can improve what you already have, or rebuild it if that serves you better.
