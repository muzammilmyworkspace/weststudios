05 / FAQBefore you ask

You’ve got questions.
We’ve got answers.

The important stuff before we start building together. Still wondering about something? Ask us directly.

01How do we start?

Start with the project brief at the end of the experience. Tell us what you’re building, where you are now and where you want to go. We’ll review it, ask the right questions and come back with a recommended direction, scope and quote.

02What can Westside help us with?

Westside works across Website Development, App Development, Performance Marketing, Creative Marketing, Branding, Social Media, SEO / AEO and Sourcing. Come to us for one discipline or bring us the bigger problem: we’ll build the right team around it.

03How long does a project take?

It depends on what we’re building. Focused projects can move in a few weeks; larger websites, apps, brands and integrated engagements take longer. We’ll give you a clear timeline before the work starts, and keep you close to the process throughout.

04How does pricing work?

We don’t force every project into the same package. Pricing is based on scope, complexity, timeline and the team required. Once we understand what you’re trying to achieve, we’ll recommend the right approach and give you a clear quote before anything begins.

05Do we need to start from scratch?

Not at all. We can build from zero, improve what already exists or step into an existing system. Websites, apps, campaigns, ad accounts, social channels and brand assets: we’ll first understand what’s worth keeping and what needs to change.

06Do you work with brands outside Pakistan?

Yes. Westside works remotely with brands across markets and time zones. Our process is built around clear communication, shared systems and regular collaboration, wherever your team happens to be.

07Can we hire Westside for ongoing work?

Absolutely. Some projects have a finish line. Others need a team that keeps building. We work on both project-based engagements and ongoing partnerships across development, creative, marketing and growth.
